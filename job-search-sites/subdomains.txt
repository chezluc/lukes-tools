How to Use Boolean Strings:
Boolean strings are special search commands you paste directly into Google. They help you uncover Production Designer jobs hidden on company career pages, startup job boards, or VC portfolio sites.

Steps:

1. Copy one of the Boolean strings below.

2. Open Google.com in your browser.

3. Paste the string into the search bar.

4. Hit Enter and explore the results.

5. Swap 'Production Designer' with other titles like 'UX Designer' or 'UI Designer' to widen your search.

Adding a location filter in your Boolean string is simple. You just add the city, state, or region in quotes at the end of your search.
